Policy Watch / Impact Rubric

How every surfaced policy item is gated, scored, and ranked · updated 2026-08-05 · back to dashboard
Principle: A loud headline must never mask a jurisdiction, stage, or relevance miss; a quiet rule change in a core topic outranks a national think-piece.
Verify rule: Bill stage, latest action, effective dates, and comment deadlines come from the PRIMARY source (legislature site, register page) every run — never from a press summary. COMMENTARY items are reported as 'per <outlet>', never as fact.

Signal classes

ClassMeaning
LEGISLATIONA bill or resolution in Congress or a state legislature.
REGULATIONA proposed/final rule or register notice from ED or a state agency/board.
AGENCYOfficial non-rule signals: board agendas, commissioner memos, guidance, budget proposals, press releases.
COMMENTARYSecondary sources: think tanks, advocacy, ed press. Context and early warning only.

Step 1 — Relevance gates

Any gate failure archives the item (logged, not surfaced). No score is computed.

GateRule
jurisdictionFederal, or one of Relay's authorized jurisdictions (CO, CT, DE, GA, IL, NJ, NY, PA, RI, TN, TX, DC, IN; CA = pending_watch, surfaces at SCAN max). Other states only via COMMENTARY when a national trend.
topic_floorMatches >=1 taxonomy topic (config/topics.yaml). Context-tier-only matches cap at SCAN unless momentum is exceptional.
livenessLEGISLATION: current session and not dead (adjourned-without-action = ARCHIVE with a one-line note if core-tier). REGULATION: comment window open, pending finalization, or effective date in the future. COMMENTARY older than 30 days: ARCHIVE.
dedup_clusterSame underlying policy event across sources (a bill + its press coverage + a think-tank take) clusters into ONE surfaced item; the primary source leads, secondaries attach as 'coverage'.

Step 2 — Impact score

Six dimensions, 0–5 each; weights sum to 100. final = Σ(weight × score ÷ 5). Unverifiable stage/deadline facts are labeled UNVERIFIED and cap the recommendation at TRACK.

DimensionWeightAnchor at 5
relay_surface30Directly regulates Relay's operations: EPP approval/accountability, cert/licensure rules, residency/apprenticeship funding, Title II, program-approval standards
momentum20Enacted/final, or passed a chamber / final-rule stage imminent; 0-1 = introduced-and-parked, think-piece
jurisdiction_depth15Federal with direct effect, or a state where Relay has deep partnerships (NY, NJ, TX, TN, IL); 2-3 = other authorized states; 1 = CA watch / national trend
ecosystem_reach15Reshapes funding or staffing across many districts Relay serves (formula rewrite, class-size mandate, salary floor)
actionability10Relay can respond NOW: open comment period, testimony window, program-design opportunity, partnership angle
signal_quality10Primary source verified this run; 0-1 = single secondary source, unconfirmed

Overrides

  • Any failed gate => ARCHIVE (name the gate).
  • UNVERIFIED stage or deadline => cap at TRACK; say what's unverified. A near-term deadline that is unverified is itself an action item: verify it FIRST this run, don't just cap and move on.
  • COMMENTARY alone can never exceed SCAN — but COMMENTARY that cites an untracked primary document (a new rule, waiver, memo, bill) is a DISCOVERY LEAD: fetch the cited primary during judgment; if confirmed, the item enters as that primary's signal class with the outlet attached as coverage. Never let the SCAN ceiling bury a lead to a primary source.
  • A VERIFIED open comment/response window <=15 business days on a core-tier item => floor of ACT (raised from TRACK, 2026-08-05 cross-family critique: urgency plus direct relevance is exactly what ACT exists for). Adjacent-tier verified windows floor TRACK.
  • Overload partition (budget-season runs): when candidates exceed what one session can judge, process core tier first, then adjacent by jurisdiction depth (NY, NJ, TX, TN, IL first), and REPORT what was deferred — silent truncation is prohibited.

Digest lanes

  • ACT — needs a decision or response (full entries)
  • TRACK — moving legislation & rulemaking, grouped by jurisdiction (compact entries)
  • SCAN — the vibes lane: agency signals + commentary one-liners, grouped Federal then states
  • MOVEMENT — items whose stage changed since last run (auto from ledger UPDATED status)
  • ARCHIVE count + audit-file pointer (never render the list)
